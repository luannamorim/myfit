Changes: start and end frames selected, cropped, scaled down, converted to an alpha mask in WebP
format; the app recolours them to match its theme. These modified files are shared under the same
license. They are generated by `tools/exercise_art.py` from pinned commits of both repositories,
and the app lists every illustrated exercise under *Settings → Image credits*.
